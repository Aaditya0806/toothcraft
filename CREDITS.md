# Image credits

## ToothCraft's own photographs

Everything in `public/images/brand`, `clinic`, `doctors`, `patients` and `results` was taken from the existing
toothcrafts.com website and belongs to ToothCraft: the logo, clinic interiors, dentist portraits, patient
photographs shown with testimonials, and before/after treatment photographs.

The images in `public/images/stock` are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) and are used under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) (free for commercial use, no attribution required — credit is
given here as a courtesy and as a record of where each file came from).

These photographs illustrate treatments and set the mood of the site. **They do not depict ToothCraft patients,
dentists or premises** and are never captioned as such.

ToothCraft's own clinics and team would be a worthwhile upgrade — see the README.
